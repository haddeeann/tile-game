How to Play

  1. Click on the tiles from the circle areas and place them on the player board.
  2. Tiles can only be placed on rows of like colors.
  3. The exception is the overflow board where any colors can be placed.
  4. Once all the tiles are placed you can play the next round.
  5. Filled rows let you move a tile to the scoreboard during the 'move tiles to scoreboard process'.
  6. Each single tile placed on the scoreboard is worth 1 point. Additional points are given when a tile is placed and there are adjacent tiles to the placed tile.
  7. Game ends when a row is filled in the scoreboard.

Highest score wins. Take turns and have fun!
